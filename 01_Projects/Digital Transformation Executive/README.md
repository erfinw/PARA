# Digital Transformation Executive

**Type:** Career

## Outcome
Build the track record, capabilities, and market positioning required to become a credible VP/Director-level Digital Transformation executive.

## Success Signals

- Demonstrable revenue impact.
- Increased digital adoption.
- Measurable operational efficiency.
- Strong strategic thinking.
- Strong people management.
- Improved English communication.
- Executive-level communication and visibility.

## Capability Focus

- Strategic thinking
- People management
- Executive communication
- English
- Digital Transformation
- S2/postgraduate development

## Next Actions

- Maintain an evidence base of transformation outcomes.
- Quantify business impact of major initiatives.
- Develop executive narratives and case studies.
- Build a deliberate learning plan.
