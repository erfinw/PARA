# North Star

## Life Direction

Build wealth without sacrificing presence or responsibility.

## 12–18 Month Ambition

- Build business income toward Rp100M/month net profit.
- Reach approximately Rp70M/month personal financial freedom.
- Become a credible Digital Transformation executive at VP/Director level.
- Spend more meaningful time with family.
- Travel more with family.
- Support my wife's goals.
- Make the home more comfortable.

## Fatherhood Standard

Be a responsible man who takes care of his family and makes his daughter proud.

## Decision Filter

Before committing significant time, money, or attention, ask:

1. Does this strengthen family presence or financial freedom?
2. Does this compound executive or entrepreneurial capability?
3. Is this aligned with the person I want my daughter to remember?
