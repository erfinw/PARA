# AI Retrieve

## Purpose
Use the Second Brain as a decision-support system, not just a document search engine.

## Retrieval Rules

1. Search relevant Projects first.
2. Search relevant Areas second.
3. Search Resources and Daily notes for supporting context.
4. Prefer recent decisions and explicit evidence.
5. Distinguish facts, notes, assumptions, and recommendations.
6. Surface contradictions instead of hiding them.
7. Include relevant `[[wikilinks]]` whenever practical.
8. If evidence is missing, say so clearly.

## Useful Questions

- What have I been thinking about recently?
- What decisions have I made about this?
- What projects are related to this topic?
- What patterns keep appearing?
- What am I neglecting?
- What should I stop, start, or continue?

A useful answer should synthesize the knowledge base rather than merely list matching notes.
