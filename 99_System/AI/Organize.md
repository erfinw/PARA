# AI — Organize

Process Inbox notes using these rules:

1. Determine whether the note is actionable, durable knowledge, ongoing responsibility, or inactive material.
2. Route it to Project, Area, Resource, or Archive.
3. Propose a clear filename.
4. Preserve links to related notes.
5. Never move sensitive information into this public repository.
6. Flag uncertainty rather than inventing context.

The goal is retrieval and decision support, not perfect filing.
