# Second Brain Rules

## 1. Capture

Capture quickly in `00_Inbox`. Do not interrupt thinking to decide where a note belongs.

## 2. Organize

Process Inbox during review. Use PARA based on the note's current usefulness, not its original source.

- Project = active outcome with a finish line
- Area = ongoing responsibility
- Resource = useful knowledge
- Archive = inactive material

## 3. Connect

Use Obsidian wikilinks such as `[[TravelUp]]` to connect related ideas. Prefer meaningful links over excessive tagging.

## 4. Distill

When a note contains durable knowledge, rewrite it into a concise, reusable form and record your own interpretation.

## 5. Decisions

Important decisions should capture context, decision, rationale, trade-offs, and review triggers.

## 6. Privacy

This repository is public. Never store passwords, tokens, credentials, private medical information, financial account details, confidential employer information, or other sensitive personal data here.

## 7. AI

AI may suggest classification, links, summaries, and questions. Human judgment remains the authority for important decisions and sensitive information.
